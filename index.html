GUIDE How to Explore This Woodland Grimoire (Controls & Shortcuts) + Explore
  • Keyboard & Swipe Navigation: Press your ← and → arrow keys (or swipe on touchscreens) to move effortlessly between cards.
  • Quick Card Jumper: Click the card badge (e.g. Start or 3 / 15) in the header anytime to jump directly to any of the 16 cards.
  • Sunlit Glade & Woodland Night: Click the glowing Pixel Sun in the top-right corner anytime to toggle between cozy dark mode and warm parchment daylight.
  • 16 Botanical Pixel Cursors: On desktop, click the Cursor button in the top-right corner to equip any of the 16 pixel flowers or 30 pixel pointers.
  • Redeem All 16 Blooms: Collect flowers across Cards 0–15 and redeem them on Card 15 for a printable & shareable Certificate of Programming Fundamentals!
