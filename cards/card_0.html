What Does It Mean to Think Like a Computer?

"Before we write code, understand what a computer is and how it thinks."

The Fundamental Truth

A computer is the most literal-minded entity you'll encounter. It does exactly what you tell it—nothing more, nothing less. No intuition. No common sense. No "figuring out what you meant."

Core Principle: Programming is translating human intentions into precise, unambiguous instructions.

The Sandwich Exercise

Imagine teaching someone who's never seen food how to make a peanut butter sandwich. You cannot skip steps. You cannot assume they know what "spread" means.

Instruction Simulator

Instruction:

Why Programs Work

Programs work because they follow three principles:

  1. Precision: Every instruction must be explicit and unambiguous
  2. Sequence: Instructions execute in order, one after another
  3. Determinism: Same instructions always produce same result
"When you understand that a computer follows instructions blindly, you realize that you are the intelligence. Your job: break down problems into steps so simple that even a literal-minded machine can follow them."

Moving Forward

Next, we'll learn the building blocks that let us give these precise instructions: variables, data types, control flow. Each concept exists to solve a specific problem in translating human thought into machine action.
